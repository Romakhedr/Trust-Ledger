## The Big Idea

Every economic relationship has **two ledgers**:

| Visible Ledger | Invisible Ledger |
|---|---|
| Price, salary, rate | Trust and reliability |
| Payment terms and dates | Goodwill and flexibility |
| Scope and deliverables | Reputation and referrals |
| Written contract | Honesty of information |

The visible ledger tells you what was agreed. The invisible ledger tells you **what will happen when something goes wrong**. This book shows you how to manage both.

## The Framework

| Stage | Chapters | You will learn to |
|---|---|---|
| **See** | 1–2 | Map your relationships and measure dependence and health |
| **Understand** | 3–4 | Know your real numbers and read the other side's interests |
| **Build** | 5–6 | Design terms that grow value and talk about money without damage |
| **Sustain** | 7–8 | Repair strained trust and review your key relationships regularly |

## The Eight Principles

1. Never win the visible ledger at the cost of the invisible one.
2. Unexamined dependence is the real risk. Map it.
3. Your power equals the quality of your alternatives.
4. Negotiate interests, not positions.
5. Create value before you divide it.
6. Be soft on the person, firm on the terms.
7. Solve problems against objective standards, and exit with your reputation intact.
8. Review quarterly, renew early, diversify calmly, and keep depositing trust.
